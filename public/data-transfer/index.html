Assumptions: NAT gateways are deployed one per AZ alongside the workloads (no cross-AZ hop to reach them); internet egress is billed at the region's tiered rates beyond the global 100 GB/month free tier, with NAT processing on top; cross-AZ counts both directions at the regional rate; cross-region is the outbound pair rate (inbound inter-region is $0.00 in the current price list). Not modeled: VPC peering / Transit Gateway hourly and processing charges, CloudFront, S3-specific egress rates, public IPv4 address charges, and endpoint policies. All prices are USD from the AWS Price List API; check the current AWS pricing pages before relying on an estimate.
